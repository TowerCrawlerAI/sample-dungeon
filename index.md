> The third floor of the Tower does not announce itself with a doorway, only with a smell: dust ground fine as cinnamon, and beneath it the wet iron tang of something that has been bleeding quietly for a very long time. Then the violet sky resolves overhead, low and bruised, and the contestants find themselves standing in a flat expanse of pale silt that drifts and reshapes around their boots. From every horizon rise the bone trees — femurs and ribs and the long curved jaws of beasts no one in the prime planes remembers — fused into trunks and branches by a slow accretion, like coral. There is no wind, but the trees make a noise anyway: a steady, distant clatter, the sound of a thousand small things settling.

## Adjudicator Notes

### Scoring Summary

> - `first_clear` (+500): first party to defeat the [Skull King](#Skull%20King) and take the [Crown](#The%20Skull%20King%27s%20Crown).
> - `hidden_objective` (+300): completing [The Hidden Name](#The%20Hidden%20Name).
> - `time_bonus` (sliding): under 6 in-fiction hours full clear, scaled.
> - `casualties` (penalty): −50 per PC death.

### The Name Mechanic

> `skull_king_true_name` is a party-flag the party can acquire two ways: pressing [The Sigil](#The%20Sigil) in the [Ossuary](#Ossuary), or correct conversation with [The Hanged Corpse](#The%20Hanged%20Corpse). If the flag is set at the start of any round during the [Throne Audience](#Throne%20Audience), the party may declare speaking the name as a free action; this consumes the flag and applies the stun.

### Optional Content

> The [Bone Construct](#Bone%20Construct) and [Spring Water](#Spring%20Water) are entirely optional. The Spring Water's fear protection is in-fiction flavor — it does not affect any sim mechanic in v0 but the chronicler should reference it if the party drinks. [The Hanged Corpse](#The%20Hanged%20Corpse) is a no-combat NPC; engaging it costs only roleplay time.
