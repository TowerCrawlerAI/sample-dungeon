# Stale Biscuit

- kind: food
- edible: true
- at_location: catacombs
- aliases: [biscuit, ration, rations, food, hardtack, crust]

> A heel of travel-bread, long since gone to stone. Someone left it wedged in an
> empty niche — one of the scavengers who worked these passages, most likely, or
> a contestant who chose flight over a last meal. It is hard as flint, gritty
> with bone dust, and tastes like nothing you can name. It will not kill you.
