# Iron Mechanism

- kind: scenery
- at_location: catacombs

- pushable: true
- turnable: true
- aliases: [lever, valve, wheel, mechanism, iron lever, iron valve, iron wheel]

> An iron assembly bolted into the ashlar near the freshly chiselled niche: part
> lever, part valve-wheel, part something with no name in common use. The chisel
> marks ring it like a frame. Whoever worked here recently left in too much of a
> hurry to use it, or else discovered it does nothing useful at all. The lever
> resists at first, then yields with a grinding clatter; the valve turns with
> great effort, hissing fine bone dust; the wheel gives a half-rotation and
> locks. Nothing moves. Nothing opens. But the mechanism clearly does something,
> somewhere, to something.
