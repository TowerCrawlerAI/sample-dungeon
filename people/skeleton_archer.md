> Lesser undead loyal to the [Skull King](#Skull%20King). They occupy the gallery above the floor of the [Hall of Skulls](#Hall%20of%20Skulls) and shoot through arrow-slits worked into the wall behind his throne. Three-quarters cover from the gallery; they emerge into the open only if the gallery floor is breached.
