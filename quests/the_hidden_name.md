# The Hidden Name

- visible: false
- reward:
  - xp: 300
  - score_bonus: hidden_objective
- discovery: found(the_sigil) OR dialogue_success(the_hanged_corpse)
- complete: killed(skull_king) AND flag(name_spoken)

> Hidden until the party discovers either [The Sigil](#The%20Sigil) in the [Ossuary](#Ossuary) or has the right conversation with [The Hanged Corpse](#The%20Hanged%20Corpse). Completion requires learning the [Skull King's](#Skull%20King) true name, speaking it aloud during the [Throne Audience](#Throne%20Audience), and surviving and winning that encounter.
