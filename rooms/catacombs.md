> The descending corridor bottoms out into a series of low, vaulted chambers
> far older than the Ossuary above. The vaulting is human work — precise
> ashlar, not bone — and the niches cut into every wall are empty, scraped
> clean by something methodical. The darkness here is complete and natural;
> it does not press the way magical darkness does, but it is total. Somewhere
> deeper, water moves.

> The lowest chamber has been disturbed recently. Fresh chisel marks ring one
> of the emptied niches, and a faint smell of char suggests someone burned
> documents here not long ago.
